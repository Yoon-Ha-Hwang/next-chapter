Come to

New England


A paradise for everyone...

... whether you are fishing, hunting, farming!

Fishers

Fish are in great quality and quantity.

Picture a dinner full of sturgeon, cod, mullets, eels, crabs, lobster, oyster, and salmon!
You can catch hundreds in a day and make several shilling a day by selling the extra fish.
As a cherry on top, you can also rest besides the pristine and clear waters of New England.

Hunters

Explore a world full of animals.

Valuable beaver, otter, and fox fur are plentiful.
You can catch hundreds in a day and make several shilling a day by selling the extra fish.
Also, be delighted by the diverse wildlife! Eagles, ducks, and turkies are just some of the many animals you can find here.

Farmers

Swaths of land are waiting for you.

No more paying hefty rent to European landlords. New England is large and scarcity is scarce.
Land is fertile and yields much more hemp, turnips, carrots, and cabbage than you'd normally expect.
